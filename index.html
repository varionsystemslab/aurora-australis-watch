How this is scored

Score = 70% geomagnetic activity (forecast Kp index) + 20% cloud cover + 10% moon brightness, plus a small bonus for locations with a dark, open southern horizon. Victorian latitudes (~37.5–39°S) typically need Kp 6+ for camera-visible aurora and Kp 7+ for a realistic naked-eye chance from most coastal spots — thresholds are approximate, not guaranteed.

Data: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (Kp index, forecasts) and Open-Meteo (cloud cover). Moon and darkness times via SunCalc.
